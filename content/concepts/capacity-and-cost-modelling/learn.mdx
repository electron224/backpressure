# Capacity and Cost Modelling

Every architecture is a bill and a ceiling. Capacity says what load it holds; cost says what the hold runs per month. Design both or discover both in production.

## What the comparison shows

Same 200 RPS against two shapes: four thin replicas behind a balancer versus one fat box. Both pass — then read the cost lines. Nodes, sizes, and request volume price differently, and the cheaper shape depends on the traffic you actually run.

## The failure mode to remember

Priced-for-peak, idle-at-night: static capacity bills the maximum around the clock. Model the traffic curve, not the peak dot — or autoscale the gap.

## Interview line

"Capacity per shape, dollars per month, both from the same run. Cheapest passing design wins the whiteboard."

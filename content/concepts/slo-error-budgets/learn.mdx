# SLOs and Error Budgets

An SLO promises a number: 99.9% of requests fast and successful. The error budget is the allowed remainder — 0.1% to spend on deploys, experiments, and bad days.

## What the overload shows

A limiter ahead of a service at 150 RPS against a 100 RPS limit: a third of requests 429 while the served stay clean. Against a 99.9% SLO that burns the monthly budget in minutes. Back the traffic under the limit and the budget stops bleeding.

## The failure mode to remember

Budget blindness: shipping through a blown budget because "errors look stable." Stable red is still red — freeze launches until the budget recovers.

## Interview line

"SLO states the promise, budget prices the risk. Spend it on velocity, halt on empty."

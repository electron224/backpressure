# Message Queues vs Streams

Direct calls couple producer speed to consumer speed. A queue in between absorbs bursts the consumer survives later — trading drops for delay.

## What the burst shows

At 250 RPS against a 200 RPS service, the direct variant sheds the excess while the queued variant holds everything and drains at its own pace. Same traffic, same service: the queue converts errors into latency.

## The failure mode to remember

Unbounded patience: the queue holds until maxDepth, then drops exactly like direct — later and all at once. Depth is the backpressure signal; alert on it before the buffer becomes the outage.

## Interview line

"Queue to decouple burst from capacity; size depth from drain rate times patience, and watch lag, not just drops."

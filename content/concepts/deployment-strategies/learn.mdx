# Deployment Strategies

Every deploy is a bet. Blue-green bets the whole fleet at once with instant rollback; canary bets a sliver first and watches errors before widening.

## What the weights show

Nine-to-one split against a faulty v2: errors hold near a tenth while v1 carries on. Full cutover to the same v2 fails everything. The canary turns a fleet-wide outage into a measured tenth — with rollback one weight flip away.

## The failure mode to remember

Unwatched canary: traffic splits but nobody reads errors, so the bad version widens on schedule. A canary without error alerts is a slow-motion full deploy.

## Interview line

"Canary the blast radius, watch errors per version, widen on evidence. Rollback is a weight flip, not a redeploy."

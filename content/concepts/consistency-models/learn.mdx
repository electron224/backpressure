# Consistency Models

"Eventual" hides a menu: read-your-writes, monotonic reads, causal, strong. Each promises a specific staleness shape — pick the cheapest one your product survives.

## What the lag shows

Zero lag is strong: no stale reads ever, writes pay nothing extra. Raise lag async and staleness appears first on hot keys — reads land behind recent writes exactly when users notice: their own updates missing. Read-your-writes is the model that forbids precisely this; it costs a primary read or session pinning the lab engine grows later.

## The failure mode to remember

Assuming strong from a fast p99: latency says nothing about staleness. Measure both, promise explicitly, and never let "eventual" stand unqualified in a design review.

## Interview line

"Name the model, not the vibe: strong, read-your-writes, monotonic, or eventual — each with its staleness shape and price."

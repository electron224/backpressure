# Graceful Degradation and Bulkheads

Ships have bulkheads so one breach doesn't sink all compartments. Services need the same: shed excess at the edge, isolate the sick leg, and keep serving degraded instead of dying whole.

## What the composition shows

Limiter ahead of a breaker-guarded pool with one flaky twin. Kill the twin: the breaker ejects it, the limiter sheds the excess, and the survivor serves clean — degraded capacity, zero outage. Every mechanism here has its own concept; this is them playing together.

## The failure mode to remember

All-or-nothing: no shedding plus no isolation means one sick backend takes the fleet. Degradation is a designed state with its own alerts, not luck.

## Interview line

"Shed at the edge, isolate the sick, serve degraded. Name the degraded state before the incident names it for you."
